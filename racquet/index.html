The most addictive tennis game.

Return the ball. Beat your best. Try again.

Play 3D tennis against an opponent, with only the racquets on court. Each return adds a point and the rally gets faster. Hard, well-aimed swings earn easier replies.

On iPhone

Drag on the trackpad to move your racquet. Pull back, then swipe through the ball to hit harder and aim. Your best score stays on your device. Solo play works offline, with no ads or account.

Play solo or invite a friend through Multiplayer. Multiplayer needs internet access to find players and works best on the same Wi-Fi.
